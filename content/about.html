Our Story

CassavaForge was born from a simple idea: that a local crop like cassava can power global change. We are turning cassava starch into high-performance, biodegradable plastics.

Our Mission

Why Cassava?

Cassava is a resilient, renewable crop with immense potential. It grows well in African climates, supports local farmers and provides a reliable, low-cost source of starch for bioplastic production.
